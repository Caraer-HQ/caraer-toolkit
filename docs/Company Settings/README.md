# Company Settings

Company Settings describe the configuration that applies to the company or workspace as a whole.

## Options

The Company Settings menu can contain the following options, depending on the Caraer setup:

- **Company profile** — company name, legal name, contact details, and identifiers;
- **Branding** — logo, colours, and customer-facing identity;
- **Domains** — website and email domains used by the company;
- **Locale and time** — language, region, timezone, and date formats;
- **Notifications** — default notification preferences;
- **Billing** — billing contacts, plans, contracts, or invoice settings where enabled;
- **Integrations** — connected external services;
- **Data management** — import, export, retention, or archive settings where enabled;
- **Security and access** — company-wide access and security settings.

The exact options should follow the settings available in your Caraer workspace. Some options may only be visible to administrators.
