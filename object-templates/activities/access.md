# Activities Suite Access

Access is granted through teams. The default model is least privilege: users
can work with activities assigned to them or to their permitted suite.

## Object access

| Team | Access |
| --- | --- |
| General employee | Read; create and update own activities. |
| Recruitment | Read/create/update recruitment activities and assigned activities. |
| HR | Read/create/update HR-related activities. |
| Sales | Full access to sales and customer activities. |
| Marketing | Read/create/update marketing activities. |
| Hiring managers | Read/create/update assigned recruitment activities. |
| Management | Full access, including administration. |

## Notes

Activities inherit access from the related suite and record assignment.
