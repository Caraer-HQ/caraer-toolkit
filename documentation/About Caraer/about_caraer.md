# About Caraer

## We help companies become candidate magnets

With Caraer, you create vacancies in no time, start campaigns to attract candidates, and build a silky smooth candidate journey.

Caraer is the **Recruitment Operating System** for enterprises, growing companies, recruitment agencies, and independent recruiters. It helps organizations take control of recruitment and build or upgrade their employer brand—from careers website to candidate experience.

Caraer brings careers sites, vacancies, recruitment campaigns, candidates, communication, automation, and recruitment data together in one configurable platform. Instead of connecting separate tools and forcing recruitment into a fixed process, Caraer adapts to how an organization works.

## The recruitment journey

### Time to Live

Get your open positions online faster than ever. With features like Smart Content and VacancyMaker™, you create better content and get it online faster.

### Time to Launch

Attract the right person for your vacancies. Start attracting candidates on autopilot, request custom campaigns, or integrate your social media lead-generation campaigns.

### Time to Hire

Make your candidate journey a breeze. Build a candidate journey that moves people from first interaction to qualified candidate and successful hire.

### Time to Scale

Scale your recruitment operation without rebuilding your system from scratch. Once you have built a system that works, Caraer helps it grow with your next recruitment target.

## More than an ATS

Traditional applicant-tracking systems mainly manage applicants and hiring stages. Caraer covers the full journey from vacancy creation to campaign, hire, and scalable recruitment operations.

Caraer combines recruitment marketing, candidate management, AI, automation, configurable data, and employer-brand experiences in one platform.
