# Who Caraer Is For

Caraer helps organizations become candidate magnets, whether they are starting to hire, upgrading their employer brand, or scaling an established recruitment operation.

## Enterprise organizations

For large HR and recruitment teams that need one platform across teams, brands, departments, and locations.

- Standardize recruitment without becoming rigid.
- Connect recruitment marketing and candidate management.
- Automate high-volume qualification, follow-up, and communication.
- Build a shared recruitment data model with organization-specific flexibility.
- Scale processes and use cases without continuously adding separate tools.

## Large companies

For organizations professionalizing recruitment across a complex structure.

- Create repeatable processes for recruiters and hiring managers.
- Connect careers websites, vacancies, campaigns, candidates, and reporting.
- Keep recruitment data and knowledge inside the organization.
- Adapt workflows and access to brands, departments, and locations.

## Small and medium-sized companies (SMEs)

For companies that want professional recruitment without building a large recruitment department.

- Create vacancies, publish them, launch campaigns, and manage candidates in one place.
- Use AI and automation for vacancy creation, communication, qualification, and follow-up.
- Build a professional candidate experience with manageable resources.
- Start with the processes needed today and expand as the company grows.

## Recruitment agencies

For agencies that run sourcing, marketing, candidate management, and client delivery.

- Get vacancies live faster.
- Launch campaigns directly from vacancies.
- Build reusable talent pools and candidate relationships.
- Configure processes for permanent recruitment, temporary staffing, executive search, or specialist recruitment.
- Deliver a clearer experience to clients and candidates.

## Independent and freelance recruiters

For recruiters who want enterprise-level recruitment technology without maintaining a large technology stack.

- Run clients, vacancies, candidates, communication, and campaigns from one platform.
- Automate administrative work and candidate follow-up.
- Look professional from the first client assignment.
- Build a reusable database and talent pools that strengthen every future search.
