# Customer Results

These customer results support Caraer’s marketing and customer conversations.

## Publication rules

- Do not replace customer names with generic labels such as “a leading company” or “a recruitment agency.” Keep the exact customer name attached to the result.
- Do not alter numbers, percentages, time periods, or outcome wording without confirming the source.
- Keep the customer category or context next to the name when it is known; for example, describe what VanDoorn specializes in rather than anonymizing VanDoorn.
- Obtain customer approval before publishing a named result on the public website or in external marketing.
- If approval is pending, agents may use the result internally as evidence but must not present it as approved public case-study copy.

## Results

VanDoorn is a specialized recruitment company. Over a 12-month engagement, Caraer supported recruitment campaigns, recruitment automation, and recruiter workflows.

**Baseline:** VanDoorn did not have a dedicated recruitment system and was working from spreadsheets and Microsoft Teams.

**Scope:** Recruitment campaigns, recruitment automation, and recruiter operations over 12 months.

- Reduced **cost per hire by more than 50%**.
- Reached **2.1 million people** through recruitment campaigns.
