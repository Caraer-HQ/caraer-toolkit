# Teams

A **Team** groups Users who share work or responsibility. Teams can make it easier to assign work, share Views, and manage access consistently.

## Use Teams for responsibility

Create a Team when people regularly work on the same type of information or process, such as Sales, Support, Finance, or Operations.

Keep Team names clear and review membership when responsibilities change.

## Teams and access

A Team may be used to determine who can view or change information. The exact access rules depend on the workspace configuration. A Team does not automatically need access to every Object or Record.

## Good practice

- give each Team a clear purpose;
- keep membership current;
- avoid broad access when a smaller Team is sufficient;
- check which Views and Automations are shared with the Team.
